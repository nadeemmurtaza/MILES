How do I send my photos?+

After you pay the deposit you upload straight from your phone in your account — one folder per trip. Prefer Google Drive? Paste a link instead.

How does payment work?+

Pay 50% to confirm your order and book a design slot. The other 50% is due only after you approve the final design. In Saudi Arabia: mada, Apple Pay, STC Pay and cards. In Pakistan: JazzCash, Easypaisa, cards and bank transfer.

How many changes can I ask for?+

As many as you like. Pin a comment on any page of your proof and we revise until you love it.

How long does it take?+

Your account shows an estimated proof date as soon as your photos arrive. We confirm printing and delivery timing when you approve.

Can I give a book as a gift?+

Yes — send a gift card by WhatsApp. They upload their own photos, and you stay a surprise until the parcel arrives.

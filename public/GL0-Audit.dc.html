Option G · design-rules audit

Option F, held to the rules.

Every page of Option F checked against the Miles Retold design rules — type, shape, colour, spacing, interaction, content and phones. Option G is the result.

Type & text

CheckFound in FIn GStatus
Two typefacesThree families: Manrope, Fraunces and JetBrains Mono for numbers.Monospace removed; numbers use Manrope tabular figures. The new hero dropped the Anton wordmark, so every page now uses only Fraunces and Manrope.Fixed
Text 12 px or larger31 labels at 10–11 px (badges, counters, chat times).Every visible label is 12 px+. Body 16–19 px, secondary 14 px. Lettering printed on the little book covers is artwork and hidden from screen readers.Fixed
Uppercase only for 1–5 wordsBook kicker and 195 labels were 6–8 words in capitals.Long lines switched to sentence case; capitals kept for short labels.Fixed
Exactly one h1—22 of 22 pages have one h1.Kept
One highlighted word per headline—No headline carries two italic highlights.Kept

Shape, colour & depth

CheckFound in FIn GStatus
Radius scale 8 / 12 / 16 / 24Corners at 6, 10, 14, 18, 20, 22, 28, 30 px.Every radius snapped to the scale; buttons and pills 12 px, cards 16 px, the biggest panels 24 px.Fixed
Soft, tinted shadowsPure-black shadows up to 85% opacity; some 1 px blurs.No rgba(0,0,0): shadows are tinted deep green (warm brown in light), blur 16 px+, opacity 50% max.Fixed
Spacing on the 8 gridGaps like 22, 34, 70, 150, 170 px.Section gaps are tokens 64 / 80 / 96 / 120 px (40 / 48 / 56 / 64 on phones); smaller gaps rounded to multiples of 8.Fixed
Logo lockup 4 : 6 : 1Icon 40 px beside a 28 px wordmark, 10 px gap.Unit = 0.28 × wordmark size: wordmark 4 units tall, icon 6 units (47 px), gap 1 unit (8 px), equal space above and below.Fixed
60 / 30 / 10 colour—Green field 60, ivory text 30, saffron accent 10. Green / red reserved for paid, due and errors.Kept
Overshoot circlesNot measured.Circles next to squares (swatches, step dots) still need the 5% optical overshoot.Open

Interaction

CheckFound in FIn GStatus
Adaptive pointerA cream ring that disappeared on light photos.Faded dot (50%) + ring (38%) that invert whatever is under them; ring grows on links, steps aside over fields, off on touch and forced colours.Added
One primary button per sectionUpload had two solid buttons; Account had two.Second action turned to glass.Fixed
Undo instead of confirmRemoving a cart item or an address was instant and final.Remove shows an Undo toast for 8 seconds; nothing asks “are you sure?”.Added
Hover keeps text readable—Hover colours are locked with !important where buttons carry inline colours.Fixed
Choices travel to the builderPicking Wedding + Square then “Start” opened a blank builder.Links carry ?type=&size=&cover= and the builder opens on that choice.Added

Content & trust

CheckFound in FIn GStatus
No placeholders40+ bracket placeholders: prices, dates, numbers, reviews, policies.0 left on any page.Fixed
One price listPrices were [—] or hard-coded per page.One sample price list feeds home, books, builder, cart, checkout, order, proof, balance and gifts. Every price shows the total, 50% today and the balance together, in SAR or PKR, remembered. Labelled “Sample prices”.Added
Never invent factsTwo made-up review slots, policy filler, estimated dates.Only the real review stays, with a link to the Instagram highlight. Policies say they’re being finalised. Dates say when they’ll be confirmed.Fixed
No dead linksWhatsApp links had no number.They now open the studio contact form until the number is added.Fixed

Studio

CheckFound in FIn GStatus
Studio in the new themeAll 24 studio pages were still in the old wine-and-cream D style.Every studio page now uses the green glass, the same header logo, the radius / shadow / spacing / type rules and a light twin with a theme switch in the sidebar.Fixed
One price list in SettingsSettings → Prices had empty [—] fields and different sizes from the shop.Settings shows the same sample price list the shop uses (Portrait, Landscape, Square, in SAR and PKR).Fixed
No placeholders in the studioTotals, tracking numbers, gift-card values and reviews were [—] or “[Sample review]”.Filled with clearly labelled sample data; the one real review is used word for word.Fixed
Dead actionsThree stage buttons went to “#”.Payment link → checkout, balance link → balance page, book courier → Shipping.Fixed
Studio on a phoneThe sidebar disappeared on phones with no way to move between pages.A glass top bar with a menu of every studio page; 44 px targets; a phone “Today” page.Added
Shared inboxMessages were only inside each order.Inbox: WhatsApp, Instagram and website messages, filters, saved replies, order context, send with validation.Added
Insights for decisionsOnly a static Reports page with placeholder numbers.Six linked insight pages — overview, forecast, pricing lab, customers, operations and a decision board — with live filters, what-if sliders and saved scenarios. All on clearly labelled sample data.Added
Adaptive pointer in the studio—The studio pages don’t have the faded dot-and-ring pointer yet.Open

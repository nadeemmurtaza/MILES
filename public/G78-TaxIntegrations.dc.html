Saudi Arabia · ZATCA (Fatoora)

E-invoicing phase 2: signed UBL 2.1 XML with a QR code.

{{zChipT}}

Environment

{{zUrl}}

What it takes to go live

  1. Register the solution on the Fatoora portal and get an OTP for each device or branch.
  2. Generate a key pair and a CSR with your VAT number, then request the compliance CSID.
  3. Pass the compliance checks with sample invoices (simplified, standard, credit and debit notes).
  4. Request the production CSID. From then on every invoice is signed (XAdES), hashed and chained (ICV, PIH).
  5. Simplified (B2C) invoices are reported within 24 hours. Standard (B2B) invoices are cleared by ZATCA before you hand them over.

Pakistan · FBR Digital Invoicing

Invoices posted as JSON; FBR returns the IRN and QR.

{{fChipT}}

Environment

{{fUrl}}

What it takes to go live

  1. Register for the FBR Digital Invoicing system with your NTN and get the security token from the IRIS portal.
  2. Test in the sandbox: validate each invoice scenario, then post it.
  3. Move to production once FBR has approved your test scenarios.
  4. Every invoice is posted as JSON. FBR answers with the IRN and the QR code to print.
  5. Invoices that fail validation come back with an error code and can be corrected and resent.

ZATCAPOST /compliance/invoicesCheck a signed sample invoice

Paths are from the public technical documents at the time of writing. Check the current ZATCA and FBR documentation before building the live connection.

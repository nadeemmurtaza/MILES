Option F · audit of E8

What we fixed, and why.

E8 checked against the laws of UX, WCAG 2.2 AA, the order workflow, and every link, dead end and empty space. Option F is the result: the full customer site in the green-glass flow.

UX laws & design principles

CheckFound in E8In FStatus
Hick’s lawToo many equal choices in the dock and hero.Nav keeps 5 links + 1 action; dock cut to 4 utilities (Track, WhatsApp, Pause motion, Top).Fixed
Fitts’s lawReview dots were 10 px; footer links 16 px tall.Every control is 44 px+, dots have a 34 px hit area, footer links 36 px.Fixed
Jakob’s lawInner pages had no way back except the logo.Cart + account top-right, breadcrumbs on every inner page, logo returns home.Fixed
Von Restorff · one primary per viewGift-card button competed with “Start your book”.One solid button per view; secondary actions are glass.Fixed
Law of similarityOutline type meant both “decoration” and “clickable” (book types).Outline is decoration only; unselected types are solid at 50%.Fixed
Goal-gradient effectNo sense of progress across the order.Progress lines everywhere: Design → Checkout → Upload bar, order stages with plane, upload counter, step line on home.Added
Miller’s law · chunkingHome ran ~22 screens; scroll-locked parts felt long.Scroll-locked parts shortened by ~1,300 px; photo field tightened; content in 4-item chunks.Fixed
Doherty thresholdMotion loops ran even when hidden.Animations pause when the tab is hidden or motion is paused; scroll work batched per frame.Fixed
Tesler’s & Postel’s lawForms had no validation at all.Kind inline validation on checkout, sign-in, help, settings, tracking lookup, newsletter and discount code; phones accept spaces and dashes.Added
Peak–end ruleEnding of the purchase was a plain page.Confirmation seal + confetti, clear next step, handwritten-note promise.Added
Zeigarnik effect—Account opens on “Action needed: proof round 2” with the order stage line.Kept

Accessibility · WCAG 2.2 AA

CheckFound in E8In FStatus
1.3.1 StructureHome had 6 headings; most parts were paragraphs.Every part has a real heading; one h1 per page; main, nav and footer landmarks; skip link.Fixed
1.4.3 ContrastFaded words at 42% opacity (~2.6:1).Raised to 70% (~5:1); body text 78%.Fixed
2.2.2 Pause, stop, hideReviews and marquees moved with no way to stop.Pause on reviews, global “Pause motion” in footer and dock (remembered), reduced-motion respected.Fixed
4.1.2 Name, role, valueBook types used tab roles with no panels.Toggle buttons with aria-pressed; real tabs only where panels exist (account).Fixed
4.1.3 Status messagesChanges to panels and totals were silent.Live regions on book panel, steps, cart code, upload count, form errors.Added
2.4.7 Focus visible—2.5 px saffron focus ring on every link, button and field.Kept
2.5.8 Target sizeSeveral targets under 24 px.All controls ≥ 24 px (most ≥ 44 px); only inline text links are smaller, which the rule allows.Fixed
1.3.5 Input purpose—autocomplete on name, phone, email, address and card fields.Added
Arabic / RTLNo right-to-left support.Arabic preview in settings; a full Arabic site is the next step.Open
